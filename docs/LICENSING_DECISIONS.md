## Required decision record

Owner/counsel should record: intended use (private academic demo, self-hosted installation, managed operation or redistributed bundle), exact versions/hashes/contents, source ownership and permission, applicable terms, notices/source delivery plan, approved/blocked uses, reviewer/date and next review trigger. Do not publish privileged correspondence or consent in Git. No convenient MIT selection or assertion that GPL automatically permits/prohibits the whole planned product is made here.

Human taskLEGAL-DISTRIBUTION gives the executable review checklist. Any material unresolved data/voice/runtime/project right remains a release gate. Private models/data remain ignored; the core Docker recipe does not bundle voice/model weights. Scope must be reconsidered when adding an external platform, voice, image asset, binary or distribution channel.
