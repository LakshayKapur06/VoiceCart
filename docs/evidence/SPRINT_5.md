# Sprint 5 — safe local actions and stable references

Implemented expiring application-generated sessions/turns, result-list identity and completed-presentation gating, persistent immutable command fingerprints, a unique prepared operation per session, local cart rows and immutable committed receipts. Product records remain external owner input. Session expiry (default one hour, configurable 60 seconds–one day) and five-minute result-reference expiry are provisional prototype policies requiring owner/user review before release. Expiry cleanup deletes only session-scoped state, not catalogue data.

The cart locks the session, checks the receipt before fresh selection, guards catalogue publication, validates current facts/observed offer and aggregate quantity, updates the cart and receipt atomically, then verifies the committed receipt on a new connection. Structured cheapest recomputation also accounts for already-added quantity. Replay preserves the original price/resulting quantity even after later adds or catalogue updates. It does not claim a merchant add, inventory reservation or a current cart total from a historical receipt.

Retries retain the same operation identity. Whole transactions retry bounded serialization/deadlock/lock faults; connection loss reconciles the original receipt and otherwise reports an unknown outcome rather than success or a blind new add. Prepared commands persist across process restarts, and unresolved operations block replacement adds. Stop cancels future prepared work; a committed operation is never undone or relabeled cancelled. Raw transcripts/audio are not stored in command records.

Automated real PostgreSQL cases cover duplicates across concurrent service instances, distinct intentional adds, fingerprint/session conflicts, aggregate stock, stale offers, incomplete source claims, query-minimum replay after price changes, cancellation, receipt immutability, expiry cleanup, result presentation and stale-list isolation. Commit-reply loss, reconciliation outage and serialization faults are explicitly injected at a real database boundary; they are not power-loss or physical-network-crash experiments.

`python scripts/verify.py --postgres --package` is the completion gate; 143 tests passed in the source and installed-wheel suites alongside lint, format, configured source type checks, Bandit, compilation, dependency and diff checks. The independent fresh application-schema probe retains zero products/variants; only the test database receives fabricated products.
